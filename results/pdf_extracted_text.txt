THESE ARE EXAMPLES, NOT THE PRESCRIBED ANSWER
Both groupings above are 
one defensible reading
 of the dependencies, not the required one. A team that queries slots
sequentially and reports five turns, or splits the coverage checks differently, or keeps a call alone because it wanted the
observation before deciding what to do next, 
has not made a mistake
. 
Where the parallel boundary falls is your design
judgement, and D2(c) marks the reasoning, not the number.
What we do require is that you 
state your dependency rule, measure both ways, and show correctness did not move
. A team
that parallelised less than we did and explained why is on stronger ground than one that copied this page.
Two honest limits, and we expect you to find them.
 Parallel calls can 
raise
 cost when a call turns out to be unnecessary 
sequentially, an early observation might have told you to skip it. And they remove a decision point the model would otherwise
have used. Say where your dependency rule draws the line and why.
