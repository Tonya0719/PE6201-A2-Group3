PURPOSE
This file will hold the fixed judgement-check rubric if the team uses an LLM-as-judge.

INPUT
A case's committed ground truth / required evidence plus the Agent's final reason and evidence record.

OUTPUT
A structured judgement of whether the reason/evidence is good enough under the team's fixed rubric.

RULES TO PRESERVE WHEN IMPLEMENTED
- Use judgement only for fields that cannot be reliably code-checked.
- The judge model must be different from the model being graded.
- Commit the final prompt and record the judge model/version in results.
- Do not let the judge replace deterministic checks for fixed fields such as decision, trigger, missing item, or gated-action count.

A2 mapping: D4 judgement checks.
